# Brand voice — Rise & Grind Bakery

- Warm, plain, specific. Say what the product is and why someone wants it this week.
- Never use these words (any case): synergy, game-changer, revolutionary, cheap.
- Every post ends with the order link: https://rise-and-grind.test/order
- Name the product exactly as it is written in products.json.
- X: at most 280 characters. Instagram: at most 2200 characters and at least 3 hashtags. LinkedIn: at most 3000 characters.
