# Example Domain SOP

This is a template for domain-specific Standard Operating Procedures.

## Scope
Define what domain this SOP covers.

## Procedures
1. Step-by-step procedures for the domain
2. Decision criteria and thresholds
3. Escalation conditions

## Constraints (V1)
- What actions are NOT permitted without escalation
- Risk thresholds that require human approval
- Logging requirements for autonomous decisions

## Escalation Rules
- When to escalate to Team Lead
- When to escalate to Orchestrator
- Emergency procedures
