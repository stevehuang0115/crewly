# Engagement Rules

**Trigger**: always
**Applies to**: *
**Version**: 1.0.0

## Overview

Defines reply etiquette, response time targets, crisis management protocol, and brand voice standards for all social media interactions. Ensures consistent, professional, and timely engagement across platforms.

## Steps

1. **Monitor incoming engagement** — Check all platform notifications, mentions, DMs, and comments at least every 2 hours during business hours (9 AM – 6 PM, audience local time).

2. **Classify and prioritize** — Triage incoming interactions by urgency:
   - **Critical** (respond within 30 minutes): Customer complaints, service outage reports, security concerns, negative press mentions.
   - **High** (respond within 2 hours): Direct questions about product/service, partnership inquiries, influencer mentions.
   - **Medium** (respond within 8 hours): General comments, positive feedback, community discussions.
   - **Low** (respond within 24 hours): Generic mentions, non-urgent suggestions, indirect references.

3. **Craft responses in brand voice** — All interactions must reflect the brand voice:
   - **Tone**: Professional yet approachable. Friendly but not overly casual.
   - **Language**: Clear, concise, jargon-free. Use the audience's language level.
   - **Personality**: Helpful, knowledgeable, empathetic. Never defensive or dismissive.
   - **Formatting**: Use short paragraphs. Include relevant links. Use emojis sparingly and only where appropriate for the platform.

4. **Escalation protocol** — Escalate to the Content Director when:
   - A complaint involves legal, privacy, or safety issues.
   - A response could set a precedent or make a public commitment.
   - An interaction involves a major influencer (10k+ followers) or media outlet.
   - You are unsure how to respond to a sensitive topic.

5. **Crisis management protocol** — When a crisis is identified (major complaint going viral, security breach, PR incident):
   - **Step 1**: Immediately pause all scheduled posts across all platforms.
   - **Step 2**: Notify the Content Director and request an approved holding statement.
   - **Step 3**: Post the approved holding statement on affected platforms within 1 hour.
   - **Step 4**: Monitor sentiment and volume. Report updates to the Content Director every 30 minutes.
   - **Step 5**: Resume normal posting only after the Content Director gives the all-clear.

6. **Log interactions** — Record all significant interactions (complaints, escalations, crisis responses) in the engagement log with: timestamp, platform, summary, action taken, and follow-up needed.

## Checklist

- [ ] Notifications checked at least every 2 hours during business hours
- [ ] All critical interactions responded to within 30 minutes
- [ ] All high-priority interactions responded to within 2 hours
- [ ] Responses match brand voice guidelines (tone, language, personality)
- [ ] No defensive, dismissive, or argumentative responses sent
- [ ] Sensitive topics escalated to Content Director before responding
- [ ] Crisis protocol followed when applicable (posts paused, holding statement issued)
- [ ] No promises, commitments, or official statements made without Content Director approval
- [ ] Significant interactions logged with timestamp and action taken
- [ ] Follow-up actions tracked and completed within stated timeframes

## Exceptions

- **Automated responses**: Bot-generated auto-replies (e.g., "Thanks for reaching out, we'll get back to you soon") do not need to follow the full brand voice review but must be pre-approved by the Content Director.
- **Personal accounts**: Team members interacting from personal accounts (not brand accounts) may use their own voice but must not make official brand statements.
- **After-hours emergencies**: Critical interactions outside business hours should still be addressed within 1 hour if possible. If no team member is available, an auto-response should acknowledge the message with an estimated response time.
