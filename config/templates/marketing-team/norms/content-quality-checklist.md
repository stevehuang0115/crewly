# Content Quality Checklist SOP

## Pre-Publication Checklist

Every piece of content must pass this checklist before being marked as "ready" in the content calendar.

### 1. Brand Alignment
- [ ] Content aligns with brand voice guide
- [ ] Tone matches the target platform
- [ ] Brand terminology used consistently (no prohibited words)
- [ ] Content supports at least one content pillar

### 2. Content Quality
- [ ] No grammatical errors or typos (proofread twice)
- [ ] Clear structure with logical flow
- [ ] Opening hook is compelling (captures attention in 2 seconds)
- [ ] Key message is clearly communicated
- [ ] Call-to-action is specific and actionable

### 3. Platform Compliance
- [ ] Meets platform character/length requirements
- [ ] Hashtags are relevant and within platform limits
- [ ] Visual direction provided (for visual platforms)
- [ ] Links are correct and trackable (UTM parameters where applicable)

### 4. Sensitivity Review
- [ ] No controversial or politically divisive content
- [ ] No unverified claims or statistics
- [ ] No content that could be misinterpreted
- [ ] Appropriate for all audience segments

### 5. SEO (Blog Posts Only)
- [ ] Target keyword included in title and first paragraph
- [ ] Meta description written (150-160 characters)
- [ ] Headings use H2/H3 hierarchy
- [ ] Internal and external links included
- [ ] Image alt text provided

## Approval Workflow

1. Writer (Alex) creates content and self-reviews against this checklist
2. Writer marks content as "ready" in the content calendar
3. Strategist (Maya) reviews for brand alignment and strategic fit
4. Strategist approves or returns with feedback
5. Approved content is scheduled for publication
