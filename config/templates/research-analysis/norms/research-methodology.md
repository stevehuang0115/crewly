# Research Methodology

**Trigger**: always
**Applies to**: *
**Version**: 1.0.0

## Overview

Standard methodology for conducting research and analysis. Ensures rigor, reproducibility, and objectivity in all research outputs.

## Steps

1. **Define research question** — Clearly state the question or hypothesis before beginning research. Document scope boundaries and expected deliverables.
2. **Identify source types** — Plan which source types to use: primary (official docs, APIs, direct observation), secondary (analysis reports, reviews), and tertiary (aggregated data, summaries).
3. **Collect data systematically** — Use structured searches. Document search queries, dates, and sources consulted. Never rely on a single source for key claims.
4. **Evaluate source credibility** — Assess each source for authority, accuracy, objectivity, currency, and coverage. Prefer official documentation and peer-reviewed sources.
5. **Cross-validate findings** — Every key claim must be verified against at least 2 independent sources. Flag contradictions explicitly.
6. **Analyze and synthesize** — Organize findings into themes. Distinguish facts from opinions. Quantify where possible.
7. **Document limitations** — Explicitly state what was not covered, what data was unavailable, and confidence levels for conclusions.
8. **Peer review** — Submit findings to team lead for review before finalizing. Address all feedback.

## Checklist

- [ ] Research question clearly defined and scoped
- [ ] Minimum 3 credible sources consulted per topic
- [ ] All claims cross-validated against 2+ independent sources
- [ ] Contradictions between sources explicitly flagged
- [ ] Data collection dates and methods documented
- [ ] Facts clearly distinguished from opinions and estimates
- [ ] Limitations and confidence levels stated
- [ ] Findings reviewed by at least one peer

## Exceptions

- **Time-sensitive competitive alerts** may use a single credible source with a note that cross-validation is pending.
- **Internal process documentation** may rely on internal sources only.
